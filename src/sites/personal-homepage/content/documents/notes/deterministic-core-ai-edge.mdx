<LocaleBlock locale="zh">

<ArchiveSection id="authority" title="先标记谁拥有最终决定权">

预测假设可以来自用户、历史基线或模型建议，但现金流、折现和终值计算只有一个确定性实现。界面预览与服务端持久化调用同一公式，避免两个真相。

</ArchiveSection>

<ArchiveSection id="provenance" title="版本要保存来源，也要保存缺口">

数据快照、来源引用、缺失字段、fallback 与警告都属于估值版本。只保存最终数字会让未来的复盘失去上下文。

</ArchiveSection>

<ArchiveSection id="language" title="语言模型负责把不确定性说清楚">

模型可以比较情景、总结风险和提出需要验证的问题，但不能悄悄改写折现率，也不能把代理估值描述成确认结果。

</ArchiveSection>

</LocaleBlock>

<LocaleBlock locale="en">

<ArchiveSection id="authority" title="Declare who owns final authority">

Assumptions may come from users, historical baselines, or model suggestions, but cash flow, discounting, and terminal value have one deterministic implementation shared by preview and persistence.

</ArchiveSection>

<ArchiveSection id="provenance" title="Versions preserve sources and gaps">

Snapshots, citations, missing fields, fallbacks, and warnings all belong to a valuation version. Saving only the final number destroys future review context.

</ArchiveSection>

<ArchiveSection id="language" title="Language models explain uncertainty">

A model can compare scenarios, summarize risks, and propose verification questions, but it cannot silently rewrite discount rates or describe a proxy estimate as confirmed.

</ArchiveSection>

</LocaleBlock>
