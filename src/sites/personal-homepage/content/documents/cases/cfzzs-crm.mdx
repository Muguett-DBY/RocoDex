<LocaleBlock locale="zh">

<ArchiveSection id="workflow" title="CRM 的核心是状态可信，而不是表格数量。" eyebrow="01 / 工作流">

线索、联系人、企业、跟进记录和园区空间必须在同一权限模型下协作。仪表盘只是结果，服务端数据范围才是产品骨架。

</ArchiveSection>

<ArchiveSection id="concurrency" title="阶段流转用乐观锁保护。" eyebrow="02 / 并发">

多人同时编辑时，旧版本更新会被拒绝并要求重新读取，避免后提交的人悄悄覆盖现场信息。

</ArchiveSection>

<ArchiveSection id="delivery" title="生产验收覆盖真实角色动作。" eyebrow="03 / 交付">

E2E 不只检查页面能开，还覆盖登录、业务路由、后台路由、用户创建与 CSV 导入。

</ArchiveSection>

</LocaleBlock>

<LocaleBlock locale="en">

<ArchiveSection id="workflow" title="A CRM is only as good as its trustworthy state." eyebrow="01 / Workflow">

Leads, contacts, companies, follow-ups, and spatial inventory must cooperate under one permission model. The dashboard is an output; server-side scope is the product skeleton.

</ArchiveSection>

<ArchiveSection id="concurrency" title="Optimistic locking protects stage transitions." eyebrow="02 / Concurrency">

When multiple operators edit the same lead, stale updates are rejected and refreshed instead of silently overwriting field intelligence.

</ArchiveSection>

<ArchiveSection id="delivery" title="Production acceptance follows real role actions." eyebrow="03 / Delivery">

E2E checks more than page availability: login, business and admin routes, user creation, and CSV import are exercised.

</ArchiveSection>

</LocaleBlock>
